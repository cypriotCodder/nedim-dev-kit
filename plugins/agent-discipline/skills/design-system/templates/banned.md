These are the fingerprints of default AI-generated UI. Don't use them unless this file explicitly allows one.

**Visual**
- Purple, indigo or violet gradients, or any decorative gradient on backgrounds, text or buttons
- Gradient text and "glow" blobs behind hero sections
- Big radius plus big soft shadow on every card (`rounded-2xl shadow-lg`)
- Glassmorphism or blur panels used for decoration
- Raw Tailwind palette classes (`bg-blue-500`, `text-gray-600`). Use semantic tokens only.
- Inter, Roboto, Arial or system-ui as the brand typeface; more than 2 type families
- Everything centered. Hierarchy flattened into equal-weight cards.
- Emoji as icons; mixed icon sets
- Pure #000 or #fff, or untinted grays

**Layout patterns**
- Hero → three feature cards with icons → testimonial → CTA band (the template landing page)
- Dashboards made of identical stat cards with "+12.5%" green badges
- Sidebar plus a top bar plus breadcrumbs on a 3-screen app
- Modals for things that should be inline or a sheet

**Interaction**
- Spinners for content loading (use layout-matching skeletons)
- `transition-all`, bouncy or linear easing, durations over 400ms on UI controls
- No pressed state, or a browser-default focus ring
- Toast-only error handling; disabled buttons without a reason

**Copy**
- "Unlock", "Supercharge", "Seamless", "Elevate", "Effortless", "Revolutionize", "Welcome back! 👋"
- Lorem ipsum or generic placeholder data in screens shown to the user. Use realistic domain content.
